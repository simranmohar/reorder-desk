# Why this project

Herbal Dispatch operates an online wholesale marketplace for retailers, including direct delivery in Greater Vancouver. The company's published 2026 priorities include growing sales and improving its e-commerce platform.

Sources reviewed on September 12, 2026:

- [Herbal Dispatch wholesale marketplace](https://wholesale.herbaldispatch.com/)
- [Published 2026 priorities](https://herbaldispatch.com/blogs/investors/third-consecutive-year-of-double-digit-growth-herbal-dispatch-recaps-key-milestones-for-2025)

The project explores a plausible wholesale sales workflow: identifying accounts whose buying pattern suggests that a follow-up may be useful. It does not establish that Herbal Dispatch has this problem, uses this method, lacks a CRM, or needs a replacement system. The company has not supplied requirements or data.

## Scope decision

Build one complete path: order history → explainable queue → account review → logged conversation → scheduled next step.

This gives a reviewer evidence of business reasoning, data handling, interface work, and practical delivery. The prototype can be evaluated without company access, customer data, or paid services.

## What to measure in a real pilot

First ask the team how follow-ups are currently assigned, what its current CRM can do, and which reports it already uses. If there is an unmet need, compare the time required to prepare a follow-up list and review the relevance of flagged accounts with a sales operator. Treat conversion and additional revenue as outcomes to measure later, not benefits already demonstrated by this prototype.

## Explicitly excluded from this version

Automatic outreach, a replacement company CRM, patient or medical information, live storefront scraping, commercial customer imports, and AI-generated sales forecasts. Suggested check-in text is a fixed editable starting point, not an LLM output.
